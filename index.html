01 / Flagship engine

Meet Veyra.
Look closer.

Veyra is Rootium's flagship vulnerability analysis engine. It is being developed around a deeper approach to understanding software weaknesses, their underlying causes and the evidence that supports a finding.

VEYRA.

Understand
what others
might miss.

Moving beyond surface-level pattern matching, Veyra is designed to investigate code with context, reason about security-relevant behaviour and make its analysis explainable.

02 / The approach

From a finding
to an understanding.

Meaningful security analysis needs more than a suspicious line of code. It needs the context to understand why something is wrong, when it matters and what demonstrates it.

01 / CONTEXT

See the bigger picture

Examine relationships across code, data and execution rather than treating every finding in isolation.

02 / REASONING

Investigate the cause

Trace security-relevant conditions towards an explanation of the underlying weakness.

03 / EVIDENCE

Make analysis inspectable

Connect conclusions to code context and supporting evidence researchers can examine.

04 / VALIDATION

Test the conclusion

Separate promising candidates from findings supported by explicit validation.

03 / Why Rootium

Better questions.
Stronger security.

The hardest vulnerabilities are rarely obvious. They can emerge from assumptions, interactions and conditions distributed across a system.

Rootium is an independent security research and engineering initiative focused on building systems that help researchers investigate those deeper relationships, reason about what they find and establish evidence that can withstand scrutiny.

Our direction is simple: make complex vulnerability analysis more rigorous, explainable and useful.
